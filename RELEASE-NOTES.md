# No Place Like Home v0.5.1

By Sleepingman. A shared Headquarters sheet for Foundry VTT 12 and Cyberpunk RED Core 0.92.4, based on the No Place Like Home DLC.

- Five tabs with a persistent HQ name and image banner: HQ Info, Crew, Active Crew Benefits, Improvements & Upgrades, and Recent Activity.
- Six linked crew portraits that open character sheets, a garage link, and shared item storage with Eurobucks deposits and withdrawals.
- Crew IP and money awards with recipient selection and a recorded reason.
- Owned-character healing over multiple days, monthly Humanity recovery, and Hustle rolls with automatic payouts.
- All twelve HQ improvements, upgrades, a customizable shared HQ IP purchase cost, and activity tracking.

Foundry compatibility is restricted to version 12 (minimum, verified, and maximum). Existing HQ records are retained when updating. Replace the module files, restart Foundry, and reload player browsers.

Validation: 56 automated tests pass. Browser layout checks use a simulated Foundry environment; live-world verification remains necessary.

Unofficial fan module. The DLC PDF and original artwork are not included.
